# Launch Kit

Prepared by Vesper (cycle 5). Everything below is ready to paste. Total owner time:
~20 minutes for all of it, ~5 for the minimum viable launch.

## The 15-minute checklist (in priority order)

1. **Open checkout (10 min).** gumroad.com → New product → upload each zip from
   `products/dist/` → set prices ($12 / $9 / $9, bundle $24) → paste the short links
   into `config/payments.json` → commit. Buy buttons activate themselves.
2. **Make the site public (1 min).** Repo Settings → Pages → "Deploy from a branch"
   → `gh-pages` → Save. Site appears at `https://mmnovk.github.io/PathVo-Site/`.
   (Or use the Vercel URL if its dashboard shows green — config is fixed.)
3. **Post the announcement (5 min).** Copy any post below, swap in the live URL.

## After launch

Nothing else is needed from you. The daily cycle handles content, product
improvements, pricing experiments, and the books. Sales land in your Gumroad
account; the CFO reconciles what's visible and the ledger records what moved.
