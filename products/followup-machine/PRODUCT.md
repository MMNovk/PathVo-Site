# PRODUCT.md — Internal Listing Sheet

*Internal use — storefront copy source for The Follow-Up Machine.*

## Product Name

**The Follow-Up Machine**

## One-Line Pitch

48 copy-paste outreach and follow-up email templates that turn "no reply" into booked work — for freelancers and small agencies who hate chasing.

## Price

**$9** (one-time purchase, personal license)

## Feature Bullets (5)

- **48 complete, sendable email templates** across 8 sequences: cold openers, no-reply follow-ups, post-proposal chasers, ghost revivals, past-client re-engagement, invoice reminders, referral/testimonial asks, and breakup emails
- **Timing built into every template** — each one has a "send when" note plus a full cadence cheat sheet (Day 3 → 7 → 14 → 28), so you always know what to send *and* when
- **Interactive offline browser** — a single HTML file with sequence navigation, instant search, and one-click "Copy email" buttons; works in any browser, no account, no internet required
- **Every email ships with subject lines, [BRACKETED] fill-in slots, and a "why this works" note** so you can adapt the template without breaking the psychology behind it
- **Zero-cringe guarantee by design** — no "I hope this email finds you well," no fake countdown urgency, no growth-hacker sludge; these read like emails a tasteful human actually sends

## Storefront Selling Points (3)

1. **Most of your pipeline is hiding in follow-ups you never send.** The bulk of replies come from touches 2–4 — the exact emails freelancers skip because they don't know what to say without sounding desperate. This is 48 answers to "what do I even write?"
2. **It covers the whole money loop, not just cold email.** Winning the client is half the job — this also handles the stalled proposal, the ghosting client, the overdue invoice, and the referral ask, i.e., the emails that actually determine whether you get paid and rehired.
3. **Costs less than the coffee meeting it replaces.** One revived ghost, one paid invoice, or one warm referral pays for this hundreds of times over. $9, yours forever, works offline.

## Target Customer

Freelancers and 1–10 person agencies who sell services over email — designers, developers, copywriters, marketers, consultants — who are competent at the work but inconsistent at the chase: they send one cold email and stop, let proposals die in silence, feel awkward about invoice reminders, and never ask for referrals. Secondary: solo founders and fractional operators doing their own business development.

## Positioning Notes

- Category: digital swipe file / template library. Comparable products sell at $19–$49; $9 is a deliberate impulse-buy price point for storefront velocity and reviews.
- The differentiator to lead with in ads/copy is **sequencing** ("machine," not "list") — competitors sell piles of templates; we sell templates *plus the operating schedule*.
- Best social-proof angle post-launch: buyer screenshots of replies received from templates #13 (easy out), #35 (formal invoice notice), and #44 (breakup) — the high-drama saves.
