## How to Adapt the Templates (Without Breaking Them)

Every template has `[BRACKETED]` slots. Three rules:

1. **Fill every bracket with something specific.** `[SPECIFIC THING YOU NOTICED]` means an actual, checkable observation — "your pricing page takes 8 seconds to load on mobile," not "your website could be better." If you can't fill a bracket honestly, that template isn't the right one for this prospect. Pick another.
2. **Rewrite one sentence into your own voice.** Usually the opener or the closer. The structure of each email carries the strategy; one sentence of *you* makes it sound human. Don't rewrite the ask at the end — every template deliberately ends with a single, small, one-line-answerable question. Keep it that way.
3. **Never fake the real things.** Some templates use genuine scarcity ("I have one slot in March") or genuine deadlines. Only send those when they're true. Fake urgency is the fastest way to burn a warm lead — and readers can smell it.

**Making a template shorter is almost always safe. Making it longer almost never is.**

## Spacing & Cadence Cheat Sheet

Print this. Tape it somewhere.

```
COLD OUTREACH (Sections A + B)
Day 0 ......... Opener (pick 1 of templates 1–9)
Day 3 ......... Follow-up 1 — the useful nudge (#10 or #14)
Day 7 ......... Follow-up 2 — the different angle (#11 or #15)
Day 14 ........ Follow-up 3 — the proof drop (#12)
Day 28 ........ Follow-up 4 — the easy out (#13)
Then .......... One breakup email (#44–46). Then stop.

PROPOSAL SENT (Section C)
Day 2 ......... Receipt check (#16)
Day 5–7 ....... Silent-objection opener (#17)
Day 10–12 ..... Real deadline, only if real (#18)
Day 15+ ....... Smaller door (#19)
Day 30 ........ Proposal sunset (#21) → breakup (#47) if still nothing
(#20, the internal-pitch assist, slots in anytime they have a boss.)

GHOSTED MID-CONVERSATION (Section D)
+1 week ....... No-guilt check-in (#22)
+2 weeks ...... Direct ask (#23) — or #24 if you have new info
+3 weeks ...... Gentle file-close (#26)

INVOICES (Section F)
Day −3 ........ Pre-due heads-up (#32)
Day +2 ........ Friendly nudge (#33)
Day +9 ........ Firm but warm (#34)
Day +18 ....... Formal notice (#35)
(#36 slots in whenever a promised payment date passes.)

PAST CLIENTS (Section E) ... one touch per quarter, rotate #27–31
REFERRALS & TESTIMONIALS (Section G) ... within 72 hours of a win
```

**The pattern behind all of it:** touches start close together (attentive) and spread out over time (respectful). Every touch adds something new — never send "just bumping this."

## Three Habits That Make This Compound

1. **Schedule the follow-up when you send the opener.** The sequence only works if it actually runs. Snooze the thread, set a reminder, use whatever tool you already have.
2. **Track which openers get replies.** After 20 sends you'll know whether your market responds better to observations (#4–6) or proof (#7–9). Double down on the winner.
3. **Send the breakup email.** It feels like giving up. It's not — it's routinely the single highest-reply email in the entire sequence, and it keeps your future emails credible.
