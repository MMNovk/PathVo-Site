## How to Use (Any AI Assistant)

These prompts work with **Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot** — any modern AI chat assistant. No plugins, no special setup.

1. **Find your situation.** Browse the section headers, or open the HTML viewer and search ("invoice", "scope creep", "discount"...).
2. **Copy the full prompt** — everything inside the code block. In the HTML viewer, click **Copy prompt**.
3. **Fill in the `[BRACKETED]` slots** with your real details. This is the step that matters most: the prompts are engineered to extract *specific, situation-aware* output, and they do that in proportion to what you feed them. Paste real emails, real numbers, real client quotes wherever a slot asks for them.
4. **Paste into your AI assistant** and send.
5. **Iterate.** The first draft is a starting position. Reply with "shorter," "warmer," "firmer," "the client is more senior than that" — or paste the client's actual response and ask "now what?"

### Tips for better output

- **Give context once, benefit everywhere.** Start your chat by pasting a two-line description of your business ("I'm a freelance UX designer for fintech startups, typical project $8–15k") before the prompt. Everything gets sharper.
- **The honesty slots are load-bearing.** Many prompts ask for things like "MY HONEST HESITATION" or "THE HONEST 10% THAT'S ON ME." Fill those truthfully — the prompts are designed to give you an advisor, not a hype machine, and several will push back on you when you need it.
- **Save your winners.** When a prompt produces an email or clause that works on a real client, save the output. Your library of proven language compounds.
- **A note on legal prompts:** the contract/collections prompts (#29–38, #55, #59, #61) produce strong working drafts and reviews, and they say so themselves — they are not a substitute for a lawyer when real money is at stake.
