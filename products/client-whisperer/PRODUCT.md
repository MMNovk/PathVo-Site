# PRODUCT.md — Internal Listing Sheet

## Product Name
**The Client Whisperer**

## One-Line Pitch
104 copy-paste AI prompts that handle the business side of freelancing — pricing, contracts, scope creep, unpaid invoices, and saying no — so freelancers can stop losing money in conversations they were never trained to have.

## Price
**$9** (one-time, personal license)

## Feature Bullets (5)
- **104 battle-tested prompts across 8 sections** — scoping calls, pricing & negotiation, contract red-flag review, difficult clients & scope creep, getting paid, repeat work & referrals, positioning, and saying no gracefully.
- **Every prompt is situation-specific, not filler** — numbered title, one-line "use when" context, and a full copy-paste prompt with [BRACKETED] fill-in slots and built-in output structure (fallback ladders, escalation sequences, decision frameworks, role-play rules).
- **Interactive offline HTML viewer included** — dark, polished, single file: sidebar section nav, live search across all 104 prompts, one-click Copy buttons. No internet required, nothing tracked.
- **Works with any AI assistant** — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot. Plain-text prompts, zero setup, zero subscriptions.
- **Covers the full client lifecycle** — from the first vague inquiry email to the final demand letter, including the awkward stuff nobody templates: firing a client, declining a friend's discount request, answering "that's too expensive."

## Storefront Selling Points (3)
1. **It pays for itself the first time you use it.** One prompt that stops one discount request, recovers one overdue invoice, or adds one change-order fee returns the $9 many times over — and there are 104 of them.
2. **This is the co-worker freelancers don't have.** Solo workers have no sales team, no legal desk, no collections department. The Client Whisperer turns the AI they already use into all three — with the exact words for the conversations that decide whether they get paid well, paid late, or paid at all.
3. **Zero-fluff craft, visible in 10 seconds of scrolling.** No "act as an expert and write an email" filler — every prompt encodes real freelance strategy: three-tier price anchoring, kill-fee ladders, stall-response classification, the waived-late-fee move, the referral ask that actually gets answered. Buyers can see the difference before they buy.

## Target Customer
Working freelancers and independent contractors (designers, developers, writers, marketers, consultants, photographers) — especially those 0–5 years in who are strong at their craft but self-taught (or untaught) at the business side: underpricing, absorbing scope creep, chasing invoices apologetically, and saying yes to work they should decline. Secondary: side-hustlers going full-time and agency escapees setting up solo. They already use ChatGPT or Claude daily; they've never given it prompts this good.
