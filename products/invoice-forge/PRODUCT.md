# Invoice Forge — Product Listing Sheet (Internal)

**Product name:** Invoice Forge

**One-line pitch:** A complete invoice generator and payment tracker for freelancers in a single HTML file — open it, bill a client, get paid, no subscription ever.

**Price:** $12 (one-time)

**File delivered:** `invoice-forge.html` (~39 KB, works offline from a double-click, all modern browsers)

## Feature bullets (5)

- **Professional invoices in minutes** — live preview with a clean, typographic layout; line items, tax, percent or fixed discounts, notes, and payment terms, all computed to the cent.
- **One-click PDF** — print-optimized output for A4/Letter that renders only the invoice, ready to email to any client.
- **Built-in payment tracking** — Draft / Sent / Paid statuses with automatic Overdue detection, plus a dashboard showing outstanding total, paid this month, and overdue count.
- **Multi-currency** — bill in USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, or AUD per invoice, with correct symbols and formatting.
- **Your data stays yours** — everything saves instantly to local storage on the buyer's machine, with one-click JSON export/import for backups and moving computers.

## Storefront selling points (3)

1. **Pay once, invoice forever.** The invoicing SaaS you're avoiding costs $15–$30 *every month*. Invoice Forge costs $12 *once* and does the freelancer job: create, send, track, get paid.
2. **Radically private.** Zero network requests, zero accounts, zero telemetry. Your client list and your income never leave your computer — verifiable, since the whole product is one readable file.
3. **Works anywhere, forever.** No install, no updates to break it, no service that can shut down. If you have a browser — even offline on a plane — you can bill a client.

## Target customer

Freelancers and solo contractors (designers, developers, writers, consultants, photographers) who send roughly 1–15 invoices a month, feel that invoicing SaaS subscriptions are overkill, and value privacy and simplicity. Secondary: side-hustlers and very small studios that want a no-login tool the whole (tiny) team can copy around.
